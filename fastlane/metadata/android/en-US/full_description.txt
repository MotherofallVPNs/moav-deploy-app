Turn your own server into a powerful tool for open internet access, straight from your phone. MoaV Deploy helps you install, run and manage Mother of all VPNs (MoaV), a self-hosted platform with 18+ censorship-circumvention protocols and fallback routes.

Whether you're setting up access for yourself or sharing it with people you trust, you stay in control. No command-line expertise needed for everyday management.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Deploy MoaV to your own server with a guided setup, then update it from your phone.
• Manage access: add or remove users and share ready-to-use connection packages.
• Check which services and protocols are running, monitor server health and view live performance.
• Keep multiple MoaV servers organized in one app.
• Protect access to the app with your device's fingerprint or face unlock.

BUILT FOR RESILIENT ACCESS
MoaV combines 18+ connection methods, including VLESS/Reality, Hysteria2, Trojan, Shadowsocks, WireGuard and DNS tunnels. Different routes can help users stay connected when networks block particular protocols. Availability depends on your server setup and network conditions.

HELP OTHERS STAY CONNECTED
A MoaV server can also contribute bandwidth to Psiphon Conduit and Tor Snowflake, and you can donate the configs it generates directly to MahsaNet.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No MoaV account, ads, analytics or tracking. Server details and access keys stay in your phone's secure storage, and the app communicates directly with servers you control.

IMPORTANT TO KNOW
You'll need your own compatible server (such as a VPS). MoaV Deploy is a server deployment and administration app. It is not a VPN client: it does not connect your phone to a VPN or route your phone's internet traffic. To connect, use a compatible VPN/proxy client with the configurations generated by your MoaV server.
