What's new in 0.2.1
- Fixed a bug that stopped the app from connecting to your servers in this build. Connecting now works.
- Connection problems show a clear message with the likely cause, plus a Check connection option on the server screen.
- Server addresses pasted with extra prefixes (like ssh://) are cleaned up automatically.
- Roomier authentication picker when adding a server, now defaulting to your saved keys.
- Branded unlock prompt and added round app icons.
